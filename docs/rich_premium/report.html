Rich premium expiry straddle

Sell the at-the-money Nifty straddle at the close the day before expiry, but only when its premium is rich against what India VIX says the day should move. Hold it to the expiry settlement.

On Nifty from January 2023 to November 2026, a period the rule was not chosen on, it made +39.6 points a trade after costs, three times the +12.2 from selling every expiry. It traded 77 of 204 expiries and made about the same total, so it earns the same money with about a third of the trades, days in the market and tail risk.

How the rule works

  1. When. At the close of the trading day before each weekly expiry (Tuesday from September 2025, Thursday before that).
  2. What. The straddle at the strike nearest the index close where both the call and the put traded. Both legs are filled at the NSE close, which is the volume-weighted price of the last 30 minutes.
  3. Only if rich. Divide the straddle premium by the one-day move India VIX implies. Sell only when that ratio is above the 67th percentile of the previous 52 expiries, so the bar adjusts to each regime using past data only.
    ratio = (call + put) / (spot × VIX/100 × √(1/252))
  4. Exit. None. Hold to the expiry settlement: profit = premium − |settlement − strike|.
  5. Costs. 4 points a straddle for brokerage and slippage plus the option-sale STT of the day (0.15% of premium from April 2026). Every number here is after cost.

Why it should work: one day before expiry, option buyers often pay more than the move that follows. Selling every expiry collects some of that. The filter skips the days when the premium is thin against VIX and keeps the days when sellers are paid more for the same risk.

For and against

For

  • About +40 points a trade on Nifty in both 2023 to 2024 and 2025 to 2026.
  • Beats selling every expiry per trade in every year since 2022. Selling a random half of expiries did as well only 0.02% of the time.
  • Still positive at double the costs and after the April 2026 tax rise.
  • Same direction on Sensex and Bank Nifty, which played no part in choosing it.

Against

  • Flat on Nifty from 2019 to mid-2021, so the edge may belong to the current market only.
  • About 2,975 ideas were tried in this hunt. At that count the result (t = 3.07) is below the level that rules out luck (t ≈ 4.1).
  • Sensex and Bank Nifty ranges include zero.
  • It makes no more total rupees a lot than selling every expiry; the gain is the same money from fewer, better-paid trades.
  • No stop and no bought wings. A crash day like 12 March 2020 cost 692 points (₹51.9k a lot).
  • Fills are the NSE closing price, not real bid and ask quotes.

Live paper test

A forward tracker records each decision before the expiry settles in an append-only ledger, for Nifty and Sensex. It reviews after 40 rule trades: it passes if the result is clearly positive (t ≥ 2) and beats selling every expiry, and it stops early if losses pass three times the median premium.

Assumptions

Data: free NSE and BSE daily F&O files and India VIX, 2012 to November 2026. Rupees use each day's lot size. Margin for the drawdown percentages is taken as 10% of notional a lot, which is lower than broker margin for a naked straddle. This is research only. Nothing here places orders.
